Use Chat to ask questions over documents you are allowed to see. Answers are grounded in retrieved passages. ScopeRAG can still be wrong, so always open the sources.

## Start a conversation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat">
    After sign-in you land in Chat for your organization. Choose **New chat** in the sidebar for a fresh thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Use the model selector if your organization enabled more than one. Administrators choose which models appear, and groups can further limit models for regular users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask">
    Type in the composer and send. The assistant streams a reply. You can leave the page; ScopeRAG continues generating in the background and the message updates when you return.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Narrow what is searched

Open the filter control on the composer to limit retrieval to:

- Classification levels
- Categories
- Tags

Filters only hide documents. They never grant access to files outside your groups. Clear filters to search everything you are allowed to see.

<Info>
  Filters are remembered per conversation in the browser.
</Info>

## Read sources and citations

Assistant messages can include a sources carousel. Select a source to open the cited document at the relevant passage.

Use this to confirm the answer, compare wording, or follow up with a more specific question.

<Warning>
  If you do not see sources, the model may have answered without retrieval, the document may still be processing, or filters may have excluded every matching file.
</Warning>

## Conversations

The sidebar lists your threads. Open one to continue, or delete a conversation you no longer need. Theme (light or dark) is available from the account menu.
