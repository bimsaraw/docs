The audit log lists security-relevant actions in the organization: sign-ins, invites, document changes, group updates, and similar events.

Open **Admin Center** → **Audit Log**. Filter by action, actor, and date range. Open a row for details, or export the current view.

<Note>
  Audit log is a plan feature. If the page is unavailable, your current plan does not include it. Upgrade on **Plan & usage**.
</Note>

Use the log when you need to confirm who uploaded or deleted a file, who invited a member, or when a group’s categories changed.
