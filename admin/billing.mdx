Plan & usage shows credits, seats, file and storage limits, and self-serve billing.

## See usage

Open **Admin Center** → **Plan & usage** (owners and administrators). Other members can open **Billing** to view usage without changing the subscription.

Meters appear throughout the app when you approach a limit (users, files, storage, and similar).

## Upgrade to a Paid Plan

Owners and administrators can subscribe to **Starter**, **Team**, or **Business**, billed monthly or yearly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    On Plan & usage, pick Starter, Team, or Business and a billing interval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Enter your payment details to complete the subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your plan">
    Plan and credits update shortly after payment. Refresh the page if it still shows your previous plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the organization already has an active paid subscription, use the **billing portal** to change plans, update the payment method, or cancel.

Free and Enterprise are not available through self-serve upgrade.
