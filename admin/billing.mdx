Plan & usage shows credits, seats, file and storage limits, and self-serve billing.

## See usage

Open **Admin Center** → **Plan & usage** (owners and administrators). Other members can open **Billing** to view usage without changing the subscription.

Meters appear throughout the app when you approach a limit (users, files, storage, and similar).

## Upgrade with Stripe

Owners and administrators can start Checkout for **Starter**, **Team**, or **Business**, billed monthly or yearly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    On Plan & usage, pick Starter, Team, or Business and a billing interval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Checkout">
    You are redirected to Stripe. After payment you return to `/admin/billing?checkout=success`. Canceling returns with `checkout=canceled`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for sync">
    Plan and credits update when Stripe webhooks arrive. Refresh if the UI still shows the previous plan for a moment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the organization already has an active paid subscription, use the **billing portal** to change plans, update the payment method, or cancel. Checkout returns an error in that case instead of starting a second subscription.

Free and Enterprise are not sold through this Checkout flow.
